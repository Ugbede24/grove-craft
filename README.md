# grove-craft

GroveCraft is a decentralized social impact platform that revolutionizes community funding through AI-powered impact prediction and automated outcome verification. The platform creates self-sustaining funding ecosystems where successful projects automatically generate seed funding for similar initiatives in other communities. Unlike traditional crowdfunding, GroveCraft employs a novel triple-validation system combining community consensus, expert reviewer assessment, and real-time IoT sensor data for transparent impact measurement. The platform features dynamic funding pools that adjust contribution requirements based on community economic indicators, ensuring accessibility across diverse socioeconomic contexts. Smart contracts automatically release funds in milestone-based tranches tied to verified impact metrics, while a unique social proof mechanism allows community members to stake their reputation alongside financial contributions. The platform incorporates a learning algorithm that analyzes successful project patterns to recommend optimal funding strategies and project modifications for maximum impact. A distinctive cross-pollination feature enables successful initiatives to spawn similar projects in other regions, creating a viral network effect for social good. The system includes dispute resolution through decentralized arbitration and community voting, while maintaining full transparency through immutable impact records. Revenue is generated through optional premium analytics services for NGOs and impact investors, ensuring platform sustainability without extracting from community funds.
